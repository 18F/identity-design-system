Connect to America.gov

America.gov only uses agency services you approve and doesn't store your personal information. Your selection lasts one year and can be changed anytime in your Login.gov account.

  • Department of State Apply for your passport

    About

    Use Travel.State.Gov to apply for, renew, or track a U.S. passport online.

    What America.gov can do for you

    Apply, renew and monitor your passport application. See your uploaded documents (but not change them).

    Information shared with America.gov

    Full name, date of birth, Social Security number, mailing address.

    Learn more about Department of State
  • Medicare Find and enroll in Medicare plans

    About

    The official U.S. government website for managing federal health insurance.

    What America.gov can do for you

    View your current benefits and help you compare other qualifying Medicare plans.

    Information shared with America.gov

    Full name, date of birth, Social Security number, mailing address.

    Learn more about Medicare
